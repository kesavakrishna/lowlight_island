# Lowlight Island playable prototype

Double-click **Play.cmd** in this workspace. It uses the portable Godot 4 downloaded alongside the project. If you move the project, open `project.godot` in Godot 4 and press F6 from the main scene or F5 from anywhere in the project. You can edit `island.gd` in VS Code.

The game opens in full screen at night. Click to start. A creature is already patrolling the clearing. First light arrives after 32 seconds. Collect planks near the crash, find the battery, haul it to the radio mast, and board at the dock after the second dawn. Use the treehouse or cave to wait out the patrol, or crouch in a bush or in the tall reeds along the swamp banks. The battery prevents sprinting and climbing. Press E away from a landmark to set it down.

| Control | Action |
|---|---|
| Mouse | Look |
| WASD | Move |
| Shift | Sprint |
| C | Toggle crouch |
| F | Toggle flashlight |
| Space | Jump (a small hop while carrying the battery) |
| G | Throw a decoy during the night |
| E | Interact, build, or use a shelter. At a tree's foot: climb. Up a tree, next to the trunk: climb down |
| Q | Up a tree, next to the trunk: climb to the higher branches |
| V | Build a small platform where you stand on a branch (two planks) |
| B | Preview a screen (one plank). Aim at dry ground or a platform, then press E to place it. Press B again to cancel. |
| T | Rotate the screen preview |
| Esc | Pause and release the cursor |
| F11 | Toggle full screen |
| R | Generate a new run after the results screen |

This is a solo Windows prototype with about six minutes on the clock per run. Walk up to a tree and press E to climb. Crouch on a leafy branch to hide. If a creature sees your climb, it can follow you onto the branch or platform. A built screen blocks sight where it covers you. You can look over it by standing up.

The island is about 420 meters across, with open lowlands, three rock escarpments, and dense groves. Walk around the exposed columns to reach the long grassy slope on the back of each cliff. The smallest saplings and palms stay at ground level. On larger trees, look for a low branch or ladder, then press E to climb or descend. Look for the on-screen climb warning if a creature follows you up. The sun and moon move across the sky, and the creatures come out after dusk. Creature howls are the only audible sounds. Swamp movement still slows the player and attracts nearby creatures.

Two shallow streams drain the swamps to the coast. Follow the open central corridor between objectives, or explore the western meadow and the grassy backs of the cliffs. A walkable canopy trunk links the giant tree near the crash to the treehouse canopy. Forest growth favors moss and algae on bark, with occasional orchids along wet banks. Sparse flying lights fade in after nightfall and disappear by dawn.
The playable forest uses the nine Forest Study models in Canopy Realism. A new run faces the edge of a rainforest belt near the crash. Follow the ferns under the hanging roots to explore it, or follow the open central corridor toward the clearing. Distant plants use simplified meshes.
